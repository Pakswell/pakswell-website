ABOUT PAKSWELL · ESTABLISHED IN 2018

Built on manufacturing.
Focused on your supply.

Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Shanghai, PAKSWELL brings together manufacturing investment, automated production and export expertise to supply wooden packaging for industrial customers.

Our focus is straightforward: the right specification, dependable supply and the documentation behind it.

INVESTED IN THE SUPPLY CHAIN

A connected network.
A coordinated supply.

Through equity investments in packaging manufacturers across China, we have built a supply network that connects production with regional warehousing.

Our group’s manufacturing bases in Suzhou, Ningbo and Cangzhou are supported by warehouses in Shanghai, Qingdao, Chongqing and Foshan. Together, they give us the flexibility to coordinate production, stock and shipment around your order.

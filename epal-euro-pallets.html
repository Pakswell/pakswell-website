Before you order

A few useful answers.

Do you accept LCL pallet orders?

Yes. Share the model, quantity and destination for an LCL or FCL quotation. Smaller shipments are welcome; freight and handling still need to be evaluated for your order.

Can I review the relevant licence and documents?

Yes. We can share applicable details directly for supplier qualification. Complete credentials are not published on the website.

Can collars be included with my pallet enquiry?

Yes. Include the collar dimensions and quantities in your enquiry. We will review compatibility and whether a combined packing plan is suitable.

What information do you need to quote?

The pallet model, quantity, destination country and port or delivery location. If you do not know the port yet, the country is enough to start.

Can I mix pallets and collars in one container?

Yes. A full 40HQ reference load is 600 EPAL 1 pallets, or 2,714 standard 1200 × 800 mm pallet collar sets. For mixed loads or EPAL 3, we prepare a separate packing plan for your quantities.

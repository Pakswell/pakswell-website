Before you order

A few useful answers.

Can I order a different colour?

Yes. Send a colour reference or physical sample with your quantity and steel thickness. We will confirm the powder-coated finish in your quotation. Screen colours are a guide, not an approved colour match.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include quantity, steel thickness, finish, destination and your required delivery timing. A dimensioned drawing and collar board details help us confirm fit. Drawings can be emailed to mark.lee@pakswell.com.

Does monthly capacity mean the hinges are ready to ship?

No. The figure is maximum production output at full capacity, not available stock. We confirm availability, production lead time and packing for your order.

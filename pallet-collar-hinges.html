Before you order

A few useful answers.

Can the hinge colour be customised?

Yes. We offer custom powder-coated colours for galvanized steel hinges. Share your required colour reference with the drawing and quantity so the finish can be confirmed in the quotation.

Can you work from a drawing?

Yes. Email your drawing with the requested dimensions, material, finish and quantity. We will review the specification before quoting.

Do you supply accessories with collars?

Yes. Include lids, corners and hinge requirements with your collar enquiry so we can review the complete configuration.

Is the illustrated hinge a technical drawing?

No. Product visuals show the general component. Use the agreed dimensioned drawing for ordering and compatibility checks.

Before you order

A few useful answers.

Can I order less than a full container?

Yes. We accept LCL enquiries as well as full-container orders. Tell us your quantity and destination so we can confirm suitable packing and freight options.

Can you match an existing collar?

Send the external dimensions, board thickness, height and hinge details. A drawing or clear photos help us confirm a matching specification.

How many collars fit in a container?

A 40HQ container can hold 2,714 standard 1200 × 800 mm pallet collar sets. We also offer mixed loading with EPAL pallets. Other specifications, accessories and packing arrangements may change the quantity; the final loading plan is confirmed for your order.

Can you supply FSC-related or heat-treatment documentation?

Yes, documentation is available for review on request. We will confirm the scope and applicability to the agreed product and order; full credentials are shared directly.
